How it works

Four steps. No phone tag, no hourly billing.

  1. 1

    Submit and pay

    A short form: contact, firm, case type, a few lines on the case. Pay by card, or request an invoice.

  2. 2

    Upload the file

    You receive a secure upload link. Send documents, photos, and the claim or complaint.

  3. 3

    Engineer review

    A licensed PE on our team, matched to the case type, reviews the file. Our AI-assisted workflow speeds up document review and drafting; every opinion is formed by the engineer.

  4. 4

    Recommendation

    Case strength, whether to inspect, and next steps, in writing within 3 business days. If you then retain Atlanta Experts, the review fee is credited.

Questions attorneys ask

Answered in the first sentence.

Who can order a desk review?

Attorneys and their staff. Tell us where the case is pending and we will confirm coverage before you pay.

Is the desk review signed or sealed?

No. It is a preliminary opinion and recommendation formed by a licensed PE; a sealed report is available as a next step if the case needs one.

Will the reviewing engineer be our testifying expert?

Not necessarily. The review is consulting work, and you decide later whether to retain us and which engineer to designate.
