About the review

What is an engineering desk review?

A quick, documents-only assessment by a licensed professional engineer that tells you, in writing within 3 business days, whether the case is technically strong or weak, whether an inspection is warranted, and what to do next.

How is it different from an inspection or an expert report?

It is based only on the materials you send, with no site visit, and it is not a sealed report. Its job is to tell you whether an inspection or a retained expert is worth the cost.

What kinds of cases do you review?

Residential property claims, such as denied roof, hail, wind, water, and foundation claims, for $225; commercial, industrial, and personal injury cases for $495. See Atlanta Experts’ full expertise.

Who can order a desk review?

Attorneys and their staff. Public adjusters may also submit cases.

Engineers and testimony

Who reviews my file?

A licensed professional engineer on the Atlanta Experts team, matched to the discipline your case needs.

Is the desk review signed or sealed?

No. It is a preliminary opinion and recommendation formed by a licensed PE; a sealed report is available as a next step.

Will the reviewing engineer be our testifying expert?

Not necessarily. The review is consulting work, and you decide later whether to retain us and which engineer to designate.
